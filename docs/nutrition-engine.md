# Nutrition & Caloric Engine

## 1. Basal Metabolic Rate (Mifflin-St Jeor)
- **Men:** $BMR = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age in years}) + 5$
- **Women:** $BMR = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age in years}) - 161$

## 2. Activity Multipliers (TDEE Baseline)
- **Sedentary / Desk Job (default):** $BMR \times 1.2$ (desk workers with <5,000 steps/day)
- **Lightly Active:** $BMR \times 1.375$ (1–3 light workout sessions/week)
- **Moderately Active:** $BMR \times 1.55$ (3–5 moderate exercise days/week)
- **Very Active:** $BMR \times 1.725$ (6–7 vigorous training days/week)

## 3. Weight Loss Caloric Targets
- **Standard Deficit:** 15% to 20% below TDEE (targeting ~0.5 kg to 0.75 kg loss per week).
- **Hard Floors (Non-Negotiable):**
  - Adult Male: `1,500 kcal` minimum
  - Adult Female: `1,200 kcal` minimum

## 4. Macronutrient Distribution Framework
- **Protein Floor:** 1.6 g to 2.2 g per kg of total body mass (spares lean tissue during deficit).
- **Dietary Fat:** 20% to 30% of total daily energy (hormonal health and satiety).
- **Carbohydrates:** Remaining calories allocated to complex carbohydrates with high dietary fiber (≥28 g/day for women, ≥35 g/day for men).

## 5. Desk-Job Adjustments
- Sedentary occupations have lower insulin sensitivity during work hours.
- Prioritize high-protein, moderate-fat lunches with low glycemic loads to avoid afternoon energy crashes.
- Prescribe non-exercise thermogenesis prompts: minimum 250 steps every active work hour.
